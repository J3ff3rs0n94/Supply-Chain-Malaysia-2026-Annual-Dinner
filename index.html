📅 Key Event Details

Date: 23 October 2026
Time: 5:30 PM - 10:30 PM
Venue: Grand Ballroom Level 2, Penang Marriott Hotel
Address: 55, Gurney Dr, 10250 George Town, Penang
Dress Code: Red & Black 🔴⚫

🚗 Parking Information

Payment: Scan the QR code provided during event registration at the foyer. You will be directed to an online payment portal on your mobile device. The event guest rate is a flat fee of RM10.

Exit Procedure: When driving toward the exit gate, the barrier will automatically open for validated vehicles without requiring any additional ticket or card action.

🎭 Mystery Game Alert!

Your assigned seat number will be used for special mystery game activities throughout the evening, so please take note of your seat number.

Feel free to mingle and visit colleagues at different tables, but when the Emcee signals the start of mystery game activities, please return to your assigned seat promptly!

🎉 Pro Tip: Remember your original seat number so you don't miss out on any surprises or prizes!
